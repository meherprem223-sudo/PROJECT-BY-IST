Overview & Objective

CodeCanvas 2026 brings together over 400 collegiate developers, designers, and systems architects to build real-world open-source tools for modern campus automation, sustainable municipal microgrids, and local student welfare initiatives.

Teams of up to four may register. Cloud infrastructure credits, developer sandboxes, hardware testing kits, and hot meals will be provided at zero cost thanks to engineering industry sponsorships.
